Wonderly - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Wonderly is a children's educational application designed to help children explore geography, animals, science, and other learning activities.

1. Information We Collect

Wonderly does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from users.

2. Children's Privacy

Wonderly is designed for children. The application does not require children to create accounts or provide names, email addresses, or other personal details.

3. Offline Functionality

Wonderly's educational activities operate offline. An internet connection is not required to use the application.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Wonderly does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising trackers or analytics services.

5. Data Sharing

Wonderly does not sell or share users' personal information with third parties.

6. Permissions

Wonderly does not request access to personal information, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

7. Changes to This Policy

If Wonderly's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the Wonderly developer through the contact details provided on the Google Play store listing.
